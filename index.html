Visitor flow

A Simple Visitor Journey - No App Required

Visitors use QR codes and a mobile browser. No downloads, accounts, or app stores.

Choose Parking Slot

Visitors scan the entry QR and choose an available parking slot.

Confirm Parking

At the selected slot, visitors confirm parking using plate number and reservation PIN.

Release Parking

When leaving, visitors release their parking session easily.

Find My Car

If they forget where they parked, they enter the parking code and retrieve the location.
